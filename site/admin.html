Email updates

You get an email when members reach 25, 50, 100, 250, 500, 1,000 and on; when paying members reach 5, 10, 15, 25, 50 and on; and when the database or photo storage is 70% or 90% full. Checked every hour.

Reports

Members reported from inside the app. The reported member is already hidden from whoever reported them; this is where you decide whether anything more needs doing.

Support

Mail to support@chaudharysamaj.com becomes a ticket here, and the sender gets an automatic reply with the ticket number. Your reply goes out from support@; their answer comes back into the same ticket.

Setup

One time, in the Gmail that support@ forwards to: open script.google.com → New project, replace everything with the script below, put your SUPPORT_INBOUND_TOKEN (from Render) in place of PASTE_SUPPORT_INBOUND_TOKEN_HERE, save, choose setup and press Run, and allow access. It then checks every 5 minutes for free.

Verification

Members whose Aadhaar QR is genuine (UIDAI signature checked) but whose name, date of birth or gender differ from their profile. Approve if it is clearly the same person (nickname, spelling); reject otherwise. Matching scans are verified automatically and never appear here.
