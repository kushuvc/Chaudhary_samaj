Child safety standards

Chaudhary Samaj · operated by Kush Vijay Chaudhary · Last updated 15 September 2026

Chaudhary Samaj is a matrimonial service for adults. These are our standards against child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), and against child sexual abuse material (CSAM), as required by Google Play's Child Safety Standards policy.

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation. There is no context in which it is permitted on Chaudhary Samaj, and we will remove it, terminate the account, preserve the evidence and report it to the authorities.

1. The service is for adults only

  • You must be at least 18 years old to create an account. This is stated in our terms and at registration.
  • The date of birth given at registration is checked on our servers, and a registration from anyone under 18 is refused outright. The account is not created.
  • An account we find to belong to someone under 18 is removed, along with the profile, photographs and messages.
  • The service exists to introduce adults for marriage. It is not a social network for young people, and there is no feature intended for minors.

2. What is prohibited

The following are forbidden, and an account responsible for any of them is terminated:

  • Child sexual abuse material (CSAM) in any form — photographs, video, drawings or text
  • Sexualising a minor, including in a profile, a photograph, a biography or a message
  • Creating a profile for, or on behalf of, anyone under 18
  • Grooming, solicitation, or attempting to contact a minor for any sexual purpose
  • Trafficking, or offering a minor for marriage — child marriage is a criminal offence in India under the Prohibition of Child Marriage Act, 2006, and any attempt to use this service for it is reported
  • Sharing or requesting sexual imagery of anyone, of any age, without consent

3. How to report

Every member profile in the app can be reported. Open the profile, choose Report, and pick a reason — Under 18 is one of them. You may add details. Reporting also hides that member from you immediately.

You do not need an account to report something to us. Email aurienlimited@gmail.com with the subject Child safety and we will act on it.

You can also block any member from their profile, which hides each of you from the other straight away.

4. How we respond

  • Reports are queued for human review. Anything flagged as involving a minor is treated as the highest priority.
  • Confirmed CSAE results in immediate account termination and removal of all associated content.
  • We preserve the relevant account records and report the matter to the appropriate authority in India, including the National Commission for Protection of Child Rights and the local police, and to NCMEC where applicable.
  • We do not warn an account before removing it for CSAE, and we do not restore such accounts.
