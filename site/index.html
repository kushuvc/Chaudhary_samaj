Chaudhary Samaj

Matrimony and community, in one place

A members-only app for the Chaudhary community — find a life partner, follow community events, and reach community businesses.

What the app does

Chaudhary Samaj is a mobile application for members of the Chaudhary community in India. Members create a profile, browse verified profiles of other members, and express interest in a match. When interest is mutual, the two members can message each other inside the app.

Matrimony profiles

Browse and filter members by community, education, occupation, location and family background.

Interests and matches

Send an interest, and message privately once it is accepted.

Community events

Gatherings, meetings and announcements shared across the community.

Community businesses

A directory of businesses run by community members.

Membership

The app is free to download and use with limited access. A paid membership unlocks the full member directory.

Membership is a monthly auto-renewing subscription billed through Google Play. It renews every 30 days until cancelled. You can cancel at any time from Subscriptions in the Google Play Store; access continues until the end of the paid period. Refunds are handled under Google Play's refund policy.

Availability

Chaudhary Samaj is an Android application distributed through Google Play. It is offered in English, Hindi and Gujarati.
