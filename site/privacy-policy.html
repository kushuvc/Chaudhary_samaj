Privacy Policy

Chaudhary Samaj · Last updated 15 September 2026

This policy explains what the Chaudhary Samaj app collects, why, and what you can do about it. Chaudhary Samaj is operated by Kush Vijay Chaudhary, an individual proprietor based in Bhavnagar, Gujarat, India.

Contact: support@chaudharysamaj.com — for any question about this policy, to request your data, or to have your account deleted.

Information created as you use the app

  • Profiles you view, shortlist, or send interest to, and the interests you receive
  • Matches, and the messages you exchange within a match
  • Your saved searches, filters and notification settings
  • One-time passcodes issued for signing in, and the times they were issued and used
  • Basic technical records needed to run and secure the service, such as request rate counters

Payments

Subscriptions are billed through Google Play. We never see or store your card, UPI or bank details — Google handles the payment and tells us only whether your subscription is active, and when it expires. We keep a record of your subscription status, plan and expiry date so the app knows what to unlock.

2. Sensitive information

Caste, sub-caste, religion and horoscope details are sensitive personal information. We collect them only because they are how matches are made in a community matrimony service, and only when you choose to enter them. You can leave any of these blank, edit them at any time, or delete your account entirely.

3. Why we use it

  • To show you matches and let other members find you
  • To run your account — signing you in, verifying your email, keeping one account per mobile number
  • To operate the paid subscription and unlock the features you have paid for
  • To keep the service safe — limiting sign-in attempts and detecting abuse
  • To contact you about your account, such as sending a sign-in code

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use it for advertising, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

4. What other members can see

Your profile is visible to other signed-in members of Chaudhary Samaj. It is not visible to the public internet and is not indexed by search engines.

Members without a paid subscription can view a limited number of profiles; the rest are hidden from them entirely, including your name and photograph. Your mobile number is shown to another member only where the app states that it will be.

Profile photographs are served from a content URL. Anyone who has that exact URL can open the image, so please do not upload a photograph you would not want seen outside the app.

6. Where your data is stored

Your information is stored outside India — the database and photographs are held in Japan, and the application runs in the United States. By using Chaudhary Samaj you agree to this transfer. We choose providers that offer appropriate safeguards for data in transit and at rest.

7. How long we keep it

  • While your account is open — your profile and messages remain so the service works.
  • Sign-in codes expire after five minutes and are cleared soon after.
  • After you delete your account — your profile, photographs and messages are removed. Records we are required to keep, such as proof of a payment, may be retained for as long as the law requires.

8. Your rights

You can:

  • See and correct your information — everything on your profile is editable in the app under Profile.
  • Delete your account — from within the app, which removes your profile and photographs.
  • Get a copy of your information, or ask a question about how it is handled, by emailing us.
  • Withdraw consent by deleting your account.

We will respond to requests within 30 days.

9. Security

Connections to the app are encrypted with HTTPS. Passwords are stored as salted hashes and never in readable form. Sign-in sessions use signed tokens that expire. Access to profile details is checked on the server for every request, not only hidden in the app.

No service can promise perfect security, but we take these measures seriously and fix problems when we find them.

10. Children

Chaudhary Samaj is a matrimonial service and is not for children. You must be at least 18 years old to create an account, and at least the legal age of marriage in India to use it for its intended purpose. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has created an account, email us and we will remove it.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page, and tell you in the app if the change is significant.
